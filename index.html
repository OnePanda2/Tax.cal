Good to know

How Tax.cal works

Most people only ever see income tax and social contributions on their payslip. But you also pay VAT or sales tax on almost everything you buy, and a large share of every fuel purchase is duty. Tax.cal combines all of these into one honest, effective rate — the real percentage of your income that becomes tax. It uses published 2026 rates, runs entirely in your browser, and stores nothing.

What's included, and how we estimate it

Direct tax (income tax, social contributions, and US state tax) uses each country's real 2026 brackets and thresholds — these are the figures on your payslip, and we compute them precisely for a single earner.

Indirect tax (VAT/sales tax and fuel duty) is genuinely an estimate: a shop receipt mixes items taxed at different rates, so we apply a reasonable effective rate to each spending category rather than pretending to know your exact basket. Each line is labelled known rate, estimate or rough so you can see how much to trust it.

  • Fuel is taxed heavily and varies by country and state — treat it as a ballpark.
  • Groceries are often zero-rated or reduced, so their hidden tax is small.
  • The country comparison converts your salary at approximate exchange rates and shows direct tax only.
Is this tax advice?

No. Tax.cal is an educational estimate to show your total tax burden at a glance. It doesn't know your full circumstances — deductions, allowances, other income, regional quirks — and it is not a substitute for a qualified tax adviser. The "ways to keep more" tips are general information, not recommendations.

How is the “you could keep up to” figure worked out?

It's a typical-case model, not a personal finding. Tax.cal measures the marginal rate on your income — the rate you pay on your next pound or dollar earned — then applies it to the legal tax-advantaged contribution limits available in your country (a pension, 401(k), RRSP, superannuation and so on). We show the upper end of that range, so treat it as a ceiling rather than a forecast.

Because Tax.cal doesn't know what you already contribute or claim, some or none of it may apply to you. If you already max out your pension, your realistic figure is near zero. It's there to show the size of the prize, not to tell you what you personally are missing.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Every calculation happens in your browser. Your salary and spending are never sent to a server and nothing is saved except an optional theme preference. Close the tab and it's gone.

Tax.cal Plus is the one exception, and only if you ask for it: on that page you can press Save to keep your review behind a private link. Even then we store no name, no email and no password, and you can delete it yourself. See the privacy page for exactly what is held.

Which countries and tax year?

Ten countries, all on 2026 tax-year rates (verified September 2026): the UK (England, Wales & NI bands), the USA (all 50 states + DC, federal + state + FICA), Canada (all provinces), Australia, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain and Italy.
