What Tax.cal is

A calculator that explains its numbers

Tax.cal estimates the tax on a salary, and the tax inside everyday spending, from published tax rules. Each result names the tax year, the version of the rules used, the date they were last checked and the assumptions behind it.

Tax.cal is not tax, legal or financial advice, and it does not know your full circumstances. It does not file tax returns, submit forms, make payments or contact a tax authority for you, and nothing it shows is a binding statement of what you owe.

Accuracy

Estimates can be wrong

Some parts of a result are calculated from the rules, such as income tax, social contributions and state or provincial tax. Other parts are estimates, such as the tax inside spending. Every result says which is which and how confident it is. Rules change, real situations are more varied than any model, and mistakes are possible.

Before you rely on a figure for a decision, such as choosing India's old or new regime, setting money aside or comparing a job offer, check it with the tax authority's own tools or a qualified professional. Tax.cal does not guarantee any outcome, refund or saving.
