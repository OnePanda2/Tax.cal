Privacy

What we store, and what we don't.

Short version: the calculator stores nothing at all. The Plus questionnaire stores nothing unless you press Save, and even then it holds no name, no email and no password — just your answers behind a private link you can delete yourself.

The calculator

Nothing leaves your device

Your salary, your spending and every figure on the results page are calculated in your browser using JavaScript that ships with the page. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Close the tab and it is gone.

The only thing kept on your device is your light/dark theme preference, in your browser's own storage. It never reaches us.

Tax.cal Plus

Only if you choose to save

While you answer the questions, everything stays on your device. If you press Save my review on the results page, we store:

  • Your answers to the questionnaire
  • The country, region, gross salary and marginal rate the review was based on
  • A snapshot of the findings you were shown
  • The date it was created and the date it expires

We deliberately do not store your name, your email address, a password, or your IP address. There is no account to create, because a saved review is reached only through its private link. That link contains a secret that we keep only as a one-way hash — meaning even we cannot reconstruct your link, and if our database were ever exposed, the records in it could not be opened or traced to a person.

Treat the link like a password. Anyone who has it can open that review.

Deleting it

You do not have to ask us

Open your saved review and press Delete it permanently. The record is removed immediately and the link stops working. There is no soft delete and no copy kept.

If you do nothing, a saved review deletes itself one year after you save it. We would rather old data disappear on its own than accumulate.

If you have lost your link, we genuinely cannot help you recover it or delete that specific review — we have no way to identify which record is yours. That is the deliberate cost of not holding your identity. It will expire on its own.

The waiting list

Your email, if you give it

If you join the early-access list, we store the email address you typed and the country selected in the calculator, so we know which markets people are writing in from. That is handled by Formspree, which processes the submission and forwards it to us.

We use it to tell you when Tax.cal Plus opens. We do not sell it, and we do not pass it to advertisers. Reply to any message from us asking to be removed and you will be.

Who else touches it

Our suppliers

  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself and, like any web server, sees the requests your browser makes for pages and images.
  • Cloudflare runs the service that stores saved reviews.
  • Formspree handles early-access sign-ups.
  • Plausible counts page views so we can see which countries people are visiting from.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on these pages.

We do not run advertising, tracking pixels or session recording. We do count visits — see below.

Counting visits

We measure traffic, not people

We use Plausible to count how many people visit and which pages they land on. We need this to know whether anyone is finding the site at all, and which countries to build for next.

It is deliberately the least invasive option we could find. It sets no cookies, creates no persistent identifier, and cannot follow you to other websites. It records the page you viewed, where you arrived from, and your rough country, device type and browser — then discards your IP address rather than storing it. Nothing it collects can be traced back to you, and we could not single you out in it even if we wanted to.

What it does not see: anything you type. Your salary, your spending and your questionnaire answers are never sent to it. Those stay on your device, exactly as described above.

Plausible is an EU company and stores its data in the EU. If you would rather not be counted at all, any browser-level ad or tracker blocker will stop it, and the site works perfectly well without it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU

You have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, object to its use, and complain to your data protection regulator.

For a saved review, the access and deletion rights are built into the page itself — opening your link shows you everything we hold, and the delete button removes it. For anything else, including the waiting list, contact us using the details below.
