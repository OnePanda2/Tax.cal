Before you start

What happens to your answers

  • Your answers stay in this browser while you answer. Everything is worked out on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to save your review at the end.
  • If you do save it, we store no identity. No email, no name, no password — just the answers and a private link only you hold. You can delete it yourself at any time, and it expires on its own after a year.
  • We ask only what changes the outcome. Every question exists because it decides whether a specific saving is open to you.
  • "I'm not sure" is a real answer. We would rather treat something as unknown than build a recommendation on a guess.
  • This is information, not personal tax advice. It tells you what to check and why. It is not a substitute for an accountant, and we have not seen your return.

What this is not

  • We have not seen your tax return, payslip or bank statements. Everything above is based on what you told us in five to ten questions.
  • Amounts assume you can actually fund the contribution or claim in question. If you cannot, the saving does not exist.
  • Rules change, and some of these have conditions we could not check from your answers. Confirm before you act.
  • For anything involving property, business structure or moving money between people, get professional advice first. Those are the ones that go expensively wrong.
