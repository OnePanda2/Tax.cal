How much tax do you really pay in Germany?

On a 55.000 € salary in Germany, about 22.351 € is taken directly as income tax (einkommensteuer) and social contributions — roughly 41%. But once you add the VAT (MwSt) and fuel duty hidden in everyday spending (about 2.182 € a year), your real effective tax rate is around 45%.

Questions

Germany tax, answered

How much tax do you really pay in Germany?

On 55.000 € a year, an estimated 45% of your income becomes tax in Germany once you count 2.182 € of VAT (MwSt) and fuel duty on top of the 22.351 € taken directly. Your exact figure depends on your deductions and spending.

What is the difference between my payslip tax rate and my real tax rate?

Your payslip shows the direct rate — about 41% here (income tax (einkommensteuer) plus social contributions). Your real rate also includes the VAT (MwSt) and fuel duty you pay whenever you spend, which most people never add up. On this salary that is roughly 2.182 € more a year.
