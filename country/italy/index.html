How much tax do you really pay in Italy?

On a 35.000 € salary in Italy, about 10.732 € is taken directly as income tax (irpef) and social security (inps) — roughly 31%. But once you add the VAT (IVA) and fuel duty hidden in everyday spending (about 1449 € a year), your real effective tax rate is around 35%.

Questions

Italy tax, answered

How much tax do you really pay in Italy?

On 35.000 € a year, an estimated 35% of your income becomes tax in Italy once you count 1449 € of VAT (IVA) and fuel duty on top of the 10.732 € taken directly. Your exact figure depends on your deductions and spending.

What is the difference between my payslip tax rate and my real tax rate?

Your payslip shows the direct rate — about 31% here (income tax (irpef) plus social security (inps)). Your real rate also includes the VAT (IVA) and fuel duty you pay whenever you spend, which most people never add up. On this salary that is roughly 1449 € more a year.

Fondo pensione (pension fund) — does it help in Italy?

Contributions up to €5,164/yr come off your taxable income — the main personal lever in Italy.
